# SkillNests Career Guide: Data Scientist

**Interest area:** Tech & Computing  
**Topic:** Career in Tech

## What is this profession?

Data scientists use statistics, programming and machine learning to turn large datasets into useful predictions, insights and decisions for businesses, researchers and public institutions.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, AI, Data Science or a related engineering field; alternatively a B.Sc. in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science or Data Science followed by an M.Sc./M.Tech.

## Key skills to build

Python, Statistics, SQL, Machine learning, Data visualization

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Carnegie Mellon University
2. Stanford University
3. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

## Who may enjoy this path?

Best suited to students who enjoy mathematics, coding, problem-solving and finding patterns in data.
