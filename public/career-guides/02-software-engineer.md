# SkillNests Career Guide: Software Engineer

**Interest area:** Tech & Computing  
**Topic:** Career in Tech

## What is this profession?

Software engineers design, build, test and maintain applications, platforms and systems across web, mobile, cloud, cybersecurity and AI infrastructure.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: B.Tech/B.E. or B.Sc. in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field. Strong programming projects and internships are highly valuable.

## Key skills to build

Programming, Algorithms, System design, Git, Problem-solving

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
2. Stanford University
3. Carnegie Mellon University

## Who may enjoy this path?

A strong option if you like building things, coding and understanding how technology works.
