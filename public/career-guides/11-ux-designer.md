# SkillNests Career Guide: UX / Product Designer

**Interest area:** Design & Media  
**Topic:** Career in Design

## What is this profession?

UX and product designers research user needs and design digital experiences that are useful, accessible and intuitive.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: Bachelor's in Design, Interaction Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Visual Communication or a related field. A strong portfolio is essential.

## Key skills to build

Visual design, User research, Prototyping, Figma, Communication

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Rhode Island School of Design (RISD)
2. Parsons School of Design
3. Stanford University

## Who may enjoy this path?

Good for students who combine creativity with empathy, technology and structured problem-solving.

> **Note:** University examples are starting points, not admissions guarantees or rankings. Course requirements vary by country and institution. Students should verify current requirements with official university sources.
