# SkillNests Career Guide: Biotechnology Researcher

**Interest area:** Biology & Healthcare  
**Topic:** Career in Biotechnology

## What is this profession?

Biotechnology researchers apply biology, chemistry and technology to medicine, agriculture, diagnostics and industrial biotechnology.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: B.Sc./B.Tech. in Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Biology or a related discipline, followed by an M.Sc./M.Tech. A Ph.D. is common for independent research careers.

## Key skills to build

Biology, Chemistry, Lab work, Data analysis, Scientific writing

## Three universities well known for this field

1. University of Cambridge
2. ETH Zurich
3. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

## Who may enjoy this path?

Good for students who enjoy biology and laboratory research more than clinical patient care.
