Doctor / Physician

Medicine combines science with a very human responsibility: helping someone when they need you most.

The role
Doctors assess patients, interpret clinical information, make diagnoses, recommend treatment and work with other healthcare professionals to improve health.

The reality of the profession

Medicine is rewarding, but it is demanding. It involves years of study, continuous learning, teamwork and situations where decisions matter. Communication and empathy are as important as memorising facts.

Course pathway

In India, the usual route is an MBBS followed by postgraduate training if you want to specialise. Other countries use different structures, so students should check the medical licensing and admissions pathway for the country they are considering.

Subjects & skills

Biology · Chemistry · Physics · Clinical reasoning · Communication · Patience · Ethical decision-making

Before choosing medicine

Try to understand the profession beyond the idea of “being a doctor”. Read about different specialties, speak with healthcare professionals where possible and learn what the training actually looks like.

Admissions, licensing and course structures vary substantially by country. Always verify current requirements with official medical schools and regulators.
