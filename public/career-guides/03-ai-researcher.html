AI / Machine Learning Researcher

For the student who wants to understand not only how AI works, but why it works.

The role
AI researchers develop and test new methods for machines to learn, reason, generate information or solve difficult problems.

What you may actually do

You might read research papers, design experiments, train models, analyse failures and write up results. Research is full of uncertainty: an idea can take weeks to test and still turn out not to work. That is part of the job.

Course pathway

A bachelor’s in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Electrical Engineering or a related field is a common start. Research-heavy roles often continue into a master’s or PhD in AI, machine learning or a related discipline.

Build these foundations

Python · Linear algebra · Probability · Calculus · Algorithms · Machine learning · Scientific writing

Three universities known for AI research

  1. Stanford University
  2. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
  3. Carnegie Mellon University

A good first step

Learn one machine-learning concept deeply, reproduce a small experiment and keep a research notebook explaining what changed and why. You will learn more from one careful experiment than from ten copied projects.

University examples are not rankings or guarantees. Check current research groups, programmes and admissions requirements on official university websites.
