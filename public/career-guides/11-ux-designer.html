UX / Product Designer

Design is not decoration. It is figuring out how something should work for a real person.

The role
UX and product designers research users, map problems, design interfaces and test ideas so digital products become easier and more useful to use.

A typical project

You might interview users, sketch a rough flow, build a prototype, test it, discover that your first idea was wrong and redesign it. The strongest designers are comfortable changing their minds when evidence says they should.

Course pathway

Common degrees include Interaction Design, Product Design, Visual Communication, Human-Computer Interaction, Design or related fields. A strong portfolio can be as important as the degree title.

Skills to build

User research · Wireframing · Prototyping · Visual design · Figma · Communication · Problem framing

Start with a redesign

Choose an app you use every day. Find one frustrating step, interview two or three people about it and sketch a better flow. Explain the problem before showing your solution.
