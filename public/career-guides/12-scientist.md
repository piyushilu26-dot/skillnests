# SkillNests Career Guide: Research Scientist

**Interest area:** Science & Research  
**Topic:** Career in Scientific Research

## What is this profession?

Research scientists investigate questions, design experiments, analyse evidence and communicate findings across physics, chemistry, biology, materials science and other fields.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: Bachelor's in the chosen science, followed by a master's and often a Ph.D. for independent research positions.

## Key skills to build

Mathematics, Experimentation, Data analysis, Scientific writing, Curiosity

## Three universities well known for this field

1. California Institute of Technology (Caltech)
2. University of Cambridge
3. ETH Zurich

## Who may enjoy this path?

Best suited to students who enjoy asking why things happen and are comfortable with long-term investigation.
