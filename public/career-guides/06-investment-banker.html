Investment Banker

A finance career built around big decisions, careful analysis and very little tolerance for sloppy spreadsheets.

The role
Investment bankers advise companies, governments and institutions on transactions such as raising capital, mergers and acquisitions and other major financial decisions.

What the work feels like

The early career can be intense. You may analyse financial statements, build models, prepare presentations and work with a team under tight deadlines. Strong attention to detail matters because small errors can change a decision.

Course pathway

There is no single required degree. Economics, Finance, Mathematics, Business, Engineering and other quantitative degrees can lead into the field. Internships and strong analytical skills are especially valuable.

Skills to build

Financial literacy · Excel/spreadsheets · Accounting basics · Valuation · Economics · Communication

Three universities known for finance/business

  1. University of Pennsylvania — Wharton
  2. New York University — Stern
  3. London School of Economics and Political Science

First step

Learn how a company makes money. Pick one public company, read its annual report and explain in plain language what drives its revenue, costs and risks.
