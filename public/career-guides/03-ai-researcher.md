# SkillNests Career Guide: AI / Machine Learning Researcher

**Interest area:** Tech & Computing  
**Topic:** Career in AI & Research

## What is this profession?

AI researchers develop and evaluate new methods for machine learning and artificial intelligence, often working on algorithms, models, experiments and scientific publications.

## Course / education pathway

Bachelor's in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics or a related field, followed by an M.S./M.Tech. and often a Ph.D. for research scientist roles.

## Key skills to build

Linear algebra, Probability, Python, Optimization, Research

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Stanford University
2. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
3. Carnegie Mellon University

## Who may enjoy this path?

Especially suitable if you enjoy advanced mathematics, experimentation and difficult technical questions.
