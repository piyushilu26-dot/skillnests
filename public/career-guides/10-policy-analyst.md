# SkillNests Career Guide: Public Policy Analyst

**Interest area:** Law & Policy  
**Topic:** Career in Public Policy

## What is this profession?

Policy analysts research public problems, evaluate evidence and help governments, think tanks or organizations design and assess policies.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: Bachelor's in Economics, Political Science, Public Policy, Statistics, Law or a related subject, followed by an MPP/MPA or relevant master's for many specialist roles.

## Key skills to build

Research, Economics, Statistics, Writing, Critical thinking

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Harvard Kennedy School
2. University of Chicago
3. University of Oxford

## Who may enjoy this path?

Useful for students interested in current affairs, economics, governance and large-scale social problems.
