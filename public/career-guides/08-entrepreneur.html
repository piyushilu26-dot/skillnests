Entrepreneurship · SkillNests

Entrepreneur / Startup Founder

There is no fixed job description. You find a problem, build something useful and persuade other people to believe in it.

The role
Founders identify opportunities, talk to users, design products, build teams, manage money and make decisions when the answer is not obvious.

The honest version

Entrepreneurship is not just pitching and having a cool idea. Much of it is listening to customers, fixing boring problems, handling uncertainty and trying again after something fails.

Course pathway

There is no mandatory degree. Business, Economics, Computer Science, Engineering, Design and many other fields can be useful. The best preparation is usually a combination of domain knowledge, practical projects and learning how organisations work.

Skills to build

Problem discovery · Communication · Product thinking · Finance basics · Leadership · Experimentation · Resilience

Three universities known for entrepreneurship

  1. Stanford University
  2. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
  3. Babson College

Start small

Do not wait for a “startup idea”. Find a problem ten students around you have, interview them and make a tiny solution. Measure whether it actually helps.

University examples reflect strong entrepreneurship ecosystems, not guarantees of startup success.
