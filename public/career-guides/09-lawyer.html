Lawyer / Advocate

Law is for people who enjoy arguments — but the professional version requires evidence, precision and responsibility.

The role
Lawyers interpret law, advise clients, prepare arguments and documents, negotiate and represent people or organisations within the legal system.

Where it can take you

You can work in litigation, corporate law, intellectual property, criminal law, public interest work, policy, compliance or many other areas. The day-to-day work can look very different across specialisations.

Course pathway

In India, students commonly pursue a five-year integrated law degree after school or a three-year LL.B. after a bachelor’s degree, followed by the professional steps required for practice. Other countries have different qualification systems.

Skills to build

Reading · Argumentation · Research · Writing · Public speaking · Logical reasoning · Attention to detail

Try this now

Take a real policy question and argue both sides using evidence. Then write a one-page position that distinguishes facts, assumptions and opinions. Good lawyers learn to do all three.

Legal qualification is jurisdiction-specific. Always verify current entry and licensing rules for the country where you intend to practise.
