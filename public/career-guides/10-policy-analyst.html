Public Policy Analyst

For students who look at a social problem and ask, “What could actually be changed — and would it work?”

The role
Policy analysts research public problems, examine evidence, compare possible solutions and communicate recommendations to governments, organisations or other decision-makers.

What the work involves

A policy analyst might study education, health, climate, trade, urban planning or technology regulation. The work often combines research, statistics, economics, interviews and careful writing.

Course pathway

Useful degrees include Economics, Political Science, Public Policy, International Relations, Statistics, Law and related fields. A master’s in Public Policy or a related discipline can be valuable for specialised roles.

Skills to build

Research · Statistics · Policy writing · Economics · Critical thinking · Communication · Data interpretation

Three universities known for public policy

  1. Harvard Kennedy School
  2. University of Oxford — Blavatnik School of Government
  3. Sciences Po

Try this

Pick one issue in your city. Describe the problem, identify who is affected, compare two possible solutions and state what evidence you would need before recommending either one.
