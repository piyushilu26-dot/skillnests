# SkillNests Career Guide: Economist

**Interest area:** Business & Finance  
**Topic:** Career in Economics

## What is this profession?

Economists study how people, firms and governments make choices and use data and economic models to understand markets, policy and resource allocation.

## Course / education pathway

Typical route: Bachelor's in Economics, Mathematics, Statistics or a related subject. Many research and economist roles prefer a master's or Ph.D.

## Key skills to build

Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Statistics, Econometrics, Research

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
2. Harvard University
3. London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)

## Who may enjoy this path?

Strong fit if you enjoy economics, mathematics, research and understanding policy effects.
