Economist

For the student who keeps asking, “Why did that price change?” and then wants to test the answer.

The role
Economists study how people, firms and governments make choices when resources are limited, using theory, data and evidence to understand economic outcomes.

Where the career can lead

Economists work in government, central banks, research institutions, consulting, finance, international organisations and universities. Some specialise in labour, development, trade, public policy, environmental economics or financial economics.

Course pathway

A bachelor’s in Economics is a natural starting point. Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science or another quantitative degree can also be useful. Research-focused careers often continue to a master’s or PhD.

Skills to build

Microeconomics · Macroeconomics · Statistics · Mathematics · Econometrics · Data analysis · Writing

Three universities known for economics

  1. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
  2. Harvard University
  3. London School of Economics and Political Science

Try this

Choose a real question — for example, why avocado prices differ between countries. List possible causes, find data and decide which explanation the evidence supports. That is the economist’s mindset.
