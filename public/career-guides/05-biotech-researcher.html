Biotechnology Researcher

Where biology meets technology — and a laboratory question can become a real-world solution.

The role
Biotechnology researchers investigate biological systems and use tools from biology, chemistry, computing and engineering to develop knowledge, diagnostics, medicines, materials and other applications.

What the work involves

Expect experiments, careful measurements, failed attempts, data analysis and lots of documentation. Depending on the field, you might work with cells, genetics, microbes, biomolecules or computational models.

Course pathway

Common starting degrees include Biotechnology, Biology, Biochemistry, Biomedical Science, Chemistry or related fields. Research careers often benefit from postgraduate study, particularly for independent laboratory research.

Useful foundations

Biology · Chemistry · Statistics · Laboratory methods · Scientific writing · Data analysis

Three universities known for related research

  1. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
  2. Stanford University
  3. University of Cambridge

Try it before committing

Read a beginner-friendly research paper, learn how scientists form a hypothesis and keep a notebook of questions you would like to investigate. Curiosity is a strong starting point.
