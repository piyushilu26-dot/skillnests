# SkillNests Career Guide: Entrepreneur / Startup Founder

**Interest area:** Entrepreneurship  
**Topic:** Career in Entrepreneurship

## What is this profession?

Entrepreneurs identify problems, develop products or services, build teams and create organizations. The path is less credential-driven than many traditional professions.

## Course / education pathway

There is no single required degree. Useful options include BBA, Economics, Computer Science, Engineering or Design, alongside entrepreneurship, accounting and marketing.

## Key skills to build

Problem discovery, Product thinking, Leadership, Sales, Financial literacy

## Three universities well known for this field

1. Stanford University
2. University of Pennsylvania (Wharton)
3. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

## Who may enjoy this path?

Best approached by combining a strong domain skill with real projects, customer research and experimentation.

> **Note:** University examples are starting points, not admissions guarantees or rankings. Course requirements vary by country and institution. Students should verify current requirements with official university sources.
