# SkillNests Career Guide: Lawyer / Advocate

**Interest area:** Law & Policy  
**Topic:** Career in Law

## What is this profession?

Lawyers interpret legal rules, advise clients, prepare arguments and represent people or organizations in legal matters.

## Course / education pathway

India: a common route after Class 12 is a 5-year integrated BA LLB, BBA LLB or BCom LLB. Graduates can also pursue a 3-year LLB after a bachelor's degree.

## Key skills to build

Reading, Argumentation, Writing, Research, Public speaking

## Three universities well known for this field

1. National Law School of India University (NLSIU)
2. University of Oxford
3. Harvard University

## Who may enjoy this path?

A good fit for students who enjoy debate, reading, writing, public speaking and analysing rules.

> **Note:** University examples are starting points, not admissions guarantees or rankings. Course requirements vary by country and institution. Students should verify current requirements with official university sources.
