# SkillNests Career Guide: Doctor / Physician

**Interest area:** Biology & Healthcare  
**Topic:** Career in Medicine

## What is this profession?

Physicians diagnose illness, treat patients and work with healthcare teams to improve health. Specializations include cardiology, surgery, pediatrics and neurology.

## Course / education pathway

India: MBBS followed by postgraduate specialization such as MD/MS. Admission is generally through NEET-UG for Indian medical colleges.

## Key skills to build

Biology, Clinical reasoning, Communication, Discipline, Empathy

## Three universities well known for this field

1. AIIMS New Delhi
2. Christian Medical College, Vellore
3. Johns Hopkins University

## Who may enjoy this path?

Requires a long training pathway and sustained interest in biology, healthcare and working with people.
