Indian Parliament — Rajya Sabha

Role

The Rajya Sabha is the Council of States and represents India's federal character. A simulation typically focuses on legislation, national policy and state interests.

How it functions

Delegates debate, use motions or amendments under conference rules, negotiate across parties and vote. The exact procedure should follow the conference background guide rather than assumptions about real parliamentary practice.

Agenda areas

  • Federalism and Centre–State relations
  • National legislation
  • Economic and social policy
  • Education, health and infrastructure

Research

Study the assigned party or representative, state interests, constitutional powers, relevant legislation and previous policy positions.

Strategy

Use coalition-building and detailed amendments. Delegates should show how national proposals affect states and different regions.

Checklist

Prepare party policy, state-specific concerns, constitutional references, evidence and likely coalition partners.
