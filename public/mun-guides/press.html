International Press / Media Corps

Role

The Press Corps reports on committee developments, interviews delegates and helps create the information environment of the conference. Its rules vary significantly by MUN.

How it functions

Journalists attend sessions, gather statements, verify claims and publish articles or updates. Some conferences allow press conferences, interviews, photography or live reporting.

What to research

  • Committee agendas and procedure
  • Key countries and delegates
  • Background facts and timelines
  • Basic journalism and attribution

Good reporting

Separate verified facts from claims, attribute statements accurately, avoid misleading headlines and seek multiple perspectives. Ask precise questions and record important quotes accurately.

Preparation

Carry a notebook or digital notes, prepare interview questions and understand the conference's media rules. Creativity is useful, but accuracy and fairness come first.
