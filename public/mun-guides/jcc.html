Joint Crisis Committee (JCC)

What it is

A JCC links two or more crisis rooms whose decisions affect one another. Delegates must manage internal coordination while anticipating the actions of the opposing committee.

How it functions

Each side receives crisis updates and takes actions under the conference's rules. Messages or directives may create effects in the other room, creating a continuous strategic feedback loop.

Research

  • Objectives of every side
  • Political and military capabilities
  • Geography and logistics
  • Alliance structures
  • Historical context where applicable

Strategy

Divide roles within your team, maintain a decision log and avoid contradictory actions. Balance short-term tactical gains with long-term political consequences.

Key skill

JCCs reward coordination and anticipation. A strong delegate thinks about how the other room will interpret and respond to each decision.
