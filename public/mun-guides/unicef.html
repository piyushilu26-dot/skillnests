UNICEF

Mandate

UNICEF works for children's rights, survival, development and protection. MUN delegates focus on practical policies for education, health, nutrition, protection and equal access.

How it functions

Delegates represent countries, debate, caucus and draft recommendations. Effective solutions identify beneficiaries, implementing agencies, financing, safeguards and ways to measure impact.

Agenda areas

  • Child protection and trafficking prevention
  • Education access and learning quality
  • Nutrition and immunisation
  • Water, sanitation and hygiene
  • Children in conflict and emergencies
  • Digital safety

Research

Know national child indicators, education and health systems, UNICEF programmes, relevant conventions and budget or infrastructure constraints.

Strategy

Prioritise scalable policies, partnerships and monitoring. Consider how proposals reach rural, displaced, disabled and otherwise underserved children.

Checklist

  • Bring child-focused statistics.
  • Know your country's policy.
  • Prepare funding and implementation mechanisms.
  • Use child-rights language carefully.
