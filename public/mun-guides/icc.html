International Crisis Committee (ICC)

Format

An ICC is a dynamic crisis simulation. The exact powers and procedures depend on the conference, so delegates should read the background guide carefully before assuming what actions are permitted.

How it functions

Delegates represent actors in a rapidly changing scenario. The dais introduces updates, while delegates negotiate, issue decisions and respond to new developments. Some conferences use public directives and private notes.

Preparation

  • Study the historical or fictional setting.
  • Map every actor's interests and capabilities.
  • Prepare several contingency plans.
  • Understand the committee's rules for crisis actions.

Strategy

Set clear objectives, maintain alliances and adapt when facts change. Think several moves ahead, but avoid unrealistic actions that ignore geography, resources or political constraints.

Skills tested

Rapid analysis, negotiation, strategic planning, writing concise directives and communicating under pressure.
