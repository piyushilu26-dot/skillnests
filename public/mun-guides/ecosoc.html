Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)

Focus: Development, economic and social policy.

Mandate and role

ECOSOC coordinates international economic, social and development work across the UN system. In MUN, delegates examine policy choices affecting growth, poverty, employment, health, education and sustainable development.

How it functions

Delegates give speeches, form working groups, negotiate draft resolutions and vote under the conference's rules. Effective debate links broad development goals to financing, institutions, measurable targets and implementation.

Typical agendas

  • SDGs and sustainable finance
  • Poverty and inequality
  • Employment and decent work
  • Education and health systems
  • Development financing and debt
  • Digital inclusion

Research

Study national development indicators, economic priorities, UN development programmes, financing constraints and partnerships. Understand the difference between a recommendation and an enforceable national policy.

Bloc building

Group around shared development challenges while respecting different income levels and national capacities. Strong clauses identify funding, technical assistance, timelines and monitoring.

Preparation

  • Prepare country economic indicators.
  • Know your country's SDG priorities.
  • Bring credible statistics.
  • Prepare two or three fundable solutions.
  • Practice compromise on implementation language.
