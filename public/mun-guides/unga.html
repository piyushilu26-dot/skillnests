UN General Assembly (UNGA)

How it functions

Delegates represent member states, deliver speeches, negotiate in blocs, write working papers and draft resolutions. Resolutions are generally recommendations rather than binding law.

Agenda

Global political, economic, social and humanitarian issues, including development, migration, disarmament, technology and global governance.

Delegate focus

Research your country's position, build coalitions, negotiate practical clauses and balance national interests with multilateral solutions.
