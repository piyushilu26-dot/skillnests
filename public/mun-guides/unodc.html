UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC)

Mandate

UNODC supports international cooperation against organized crime, illicit drugs, corruption and terrorism while strengthening criminal justice and rule-of-law systems.

How it functions

Delegates represent states, debate policy, negotiate and draft recommendations. Good solutions distinguish prevention, enforcement, prosecution, rehabilitation, international cooperation and capacity-building.

Agenda areas

  • Human trafficking and migrant smuggling
  • Cyber-enabled organized crime
  • Corruption and illicit finance
  • Drug prevention and treatment
  • Criminal justice cooperation

Research

Study national laws, enforcement capacity, relevant conventions, crime trends and cross-border cooperation. Avoid unsupported accusations about particular states or groups.

Strategy

Build blocs around practical cooperation: information sharing, training, border cooperation, victim protection, financial investigations and prevention.

Checklist

  • Know relevant international instruments.
  • Prepare prevention and enforcement measures.
  • Address civil-rights safeguards.
  • Include implementation and capacity-building.
