UNSC Crisis Committee

What makes it different?

This format combines Security Council diplomacy with fast-moving crisis updates. Delegates must make decisions under time pressure while preserving a coherent national strategy.

How it functions

Expect short speeches, rapid caucuses, directives or resolutions, crisis notes and new information from the dais. Conference rules determine how private actions and crisis updates work.

What to prepare

  • Country foreign-policy profile
  • Military and diplomatic capabilities
  • Key allies and red lines
  • Likely crisis scenarios
  • Possible de-escalation and humanitarian measures

Strategy

Prioritise objectives, communicate quickly with allies and leave room to adapt. Do not promise capabilities your country could not plausibly use. Separate public diplomacy from confidential strategy when the rules permit it.

Delegate checklist

  • Track every crisis update.
  • Keep a running timeline.
  • Know voting and veto rules.
  • Write concise, actionable directives.
  • Always consider escalation and unintended consequences.
