UN-Habitat

Mandate

UN-Habitat focuses on sustainable human settlements, inclusive urban development and adequate housing. MUN delegates examine rapid urbanisation and practical city-level solutions.

Typical agendas

  • Affordable housing and homelessness
  • Urban transport and infrastructure
  • Sanitation and waste
  • Informal settlements
  • Climate-resilient cities

How it functions

Delegates research national and municipal conditions, caucus, build blocs and draft recommendations. Strong clauses address land, finance, infrastructure, governance and inclusion together.

Research

Know urbanisation rates, housing conditions, infrastructure gaps, national programmes and financing limitations. Consider both large cities and smaller settlements.

Strategy

Offer scalable policies, public-private partnerships where appropriate, local-government capacity and measurable outcomes. Avoid assuming that one model fits every city.
