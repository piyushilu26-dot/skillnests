UNESCO

Mandate

UNESCO promotes cooperation in education, science, culture and communication/information. MUN delegates should connect proposals to access, preservation, knowledge-sharing and international cooperation.

How it functions

Delegates research national policy, make speeches, form blocs, negotiate clauses and vote. Solutions should respect UNESCO's mandate and national capacity.

Typical agendas

  • Education and literacy
  • Cultural and natural heritage
  • Science cooperation
  • Media and information literacy
  • Digital access and knowledge

Research

Study national education and cultural policies, heritage sites, scientific capacity, UNESCO programmes and relevant international conventions.

Bloc strategy

Look for shared interests in funding, capacity-building, preservation, technology and access. Include measurable implementation and partnerships.

Delegate checklist

  • Know your country's cultural and education priorities.
  • Use credible UNESCO or national data.
  • Prepare funding and monitoring clauses.
  • Keep proposals within the committee's mandate.
