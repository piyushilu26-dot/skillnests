UN Women

Mandate

UN Women promotes gender equality and women's empowerment. MUN delegates debate barriers to participation, safety, education, health, economic opportunity and political representation.

How it functions

Delegates represent states, make speeches, negotiate in caucuses and draft resolutions. Strong proposals connect rights goals with implementation, funding, institutional capacity and measurable outcomes.

Typical agendas

  • Gender-based violence prevention
  • Girls' education
  • Women's economic participation
  • Political representation
  • Digital safety and access
  • Maternal and reproductive health policy

Research

Study national gender indicators, laws, programmes, international commitments and social or economic constraints. Avoid stereotyping countries or treating all national contexts as identical.

Bloc strategy

Build consensus around practical mechanisms such as education, legal capacity, reporting, funding, services and data collection. Keep language respectful and evidence-based.

Preparation

  • Prepare country-specific statistics.
  • Know relevant international frameworks.
  • Prepare two prevention and two implementation measures.
  • Anticipate cultural and policy objections.
