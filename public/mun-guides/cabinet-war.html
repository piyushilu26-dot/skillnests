Cabinet / War Cabinet

Format

A cabinet simulation places delegates inside a national executive decision-making team. A war cabinet usually focuses on an urgent security or military crisis. Exact powers depend on the conference.

How it functions

Delegates act through portfolios such as foreign affairs, defence, finance or intelligence. They debate options, receive updates and advise the head of government or take collective decisions according to the rules.

Research

  • Constitutional structure
  • Ministry responsibilities
  • Economic and military capacity
  • Alliances and diplomatic relationships
  • Domestic political constraints

Strategy

Present options with benefits, risks, costs and likely reactions. Keep the cabinet's decisions coherent and coordinate across portfolios.

Checklist

Know your portfolio, maintain a decision timeline, prepare contingency options and communicate clearly under pressure.
