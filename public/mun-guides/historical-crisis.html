Historical Crisis Committee

Core idea

Delegates operate inside a specific historical period. The objective is not simply to predict what happened in real history, but to make plausible decisions using the information, technology and political constraints available at the time.

How it functions

The dais provides crisis developments. Delegates debate, negotiate and issue actions according to conference rules. Historical knowledge helps, but in-room information and period realism matter.

Research

  • Political leaders and institutions
  • Military and economic capabilities
  • Alliances and rivalries
  • Technology available at the time
  • Social and legal constraints

Strategy

Stay historically plausible. Build alliances, anticipate opponents and keep a timeline of decisions. Do not use modern knowledge or technology unless the simulation explicitly allows it.

Preparation

Create actor profiles, a timeline, capability matrix and several contingency plans.
