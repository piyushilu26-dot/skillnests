All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM)

What it is

AIPPM is a popular Indian MUN simulation in which delegates represent political parties rather than countries. The agenda normally concerns major Indian political, constitutional or governance questions.

How it functions

Delegates make party-based speeches, negotiate, form alliances and work toward policy outcomes according to the conference rules. Party ideology matters, but delegates should also respond to the specific agenda.

Research

  • Party manifesto and ideology
  • Recent policy positions
  • Constitutional provisions
  • Relevant legislation and institutions
  • Arguments of rival parties

Strategy

Build issue-based alliances while preserving party red lines. Use evidence and parliamentary language rather than personal attacks. Anticipate amendments and prepare rebuttals.

Checklist

Prepare a party brief, opening speech, evidence bank, constitutional references, likely allies and likely attacks from opposing parties.
