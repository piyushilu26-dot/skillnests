All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM)

How it functions

Delegates represent political parties and debate major domestic or constitutional issues. Negotiation is central because party positions can differ sharply.

Agenda

Elections, federalism, reservations, economic policy, governance, education, national security and other major Indian issues.

Delegate focus

Research the assigned party's ideology and positions, defend them persuasively and remain capable of finding cross-party compromise.
