UN Environment Programme (UNEP)

Mandate

UNEP supports global environmental cooperation and assessment. MUN delegates address environmental problems while balancing climate, biodiversity, pollution, development, technology and finance.

How it functions

Delegates research national environmental policy, speak, form blocs, negotiate working papers and draft recommendations. Solutions should identify implementing actors, financing, technology transfer and monitoring.

Agenda areas

  • Climate change and adaptation
  • Plastic and chemical pollution
  • Biodiversity and ecosystems
  • Water and waste management
  • Green finance and technology

Research and bloc building

Know your country's emissions, natural resources, environmental laws, development needs and international commitments. Build coalitions around feasible measures and differentiate between obligations, incentives and voluntary cooperation.

Delegate checklist

  • Bring credible environmental data.
  • Know major international frameworks.
  • Prepare financing and implementation clauses.
  • Consider developing-country capacity constraints.
  • Offer measurable targets where appropriate.

Common mistake

Avoid solutions that ignore cost, technology access or national capacity.
