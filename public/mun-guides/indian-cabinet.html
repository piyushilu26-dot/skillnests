Indian Cabinet Simulation

Format

Delegates simulate executive decision-making within India's government. The exact roles and powers are conference-specific, so the background guide should take priority.

How it functions

Delegates may receive ministry portfolios and respond collectively to policy or crisis developments. They evaluate options, negotiate across ministries and advise or decide according to the rules.

Research

  • Union government structure
  • Ministry responsibilities
  • Constitutional and statutory powers
  • Budget and implementation constraints
  • Political and federal considerations

Strategy

Present decisions with costs, benefits, legal basis, implementation steps and likely political consequences. Coordinate ministries so that proposals do not contradict one another.

Preparation

Know your portfolio, prepare a policy brief, track developments and maintain multiple contingency options.
