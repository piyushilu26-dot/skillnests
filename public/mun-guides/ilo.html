International Labour Organization (ILO)

Mandate

The ILO promotes decent work, labour rights, employment and social protection. Its distinctive tripartite model brings governments, employers and workers into dialogue.

How it functions

In MUN, delegates debate labour policy, negotiate recommendations and seek balanced outcomes. Proposals should consider workers' rights, employer feasibility and government capacity.

Agenda areas

  • Minimum standards and decent work
  • Child and forced labour
  • Informal employment
  • Automation and future jobs
  • Social protection

Research

Study national labour laws, employment data, social-security systems, ILO conventions and economic structure.

Strategy

Build consensus across worker, employer and government perspectives. Strong clauses combine standards with implementation support, training, inspection and social dialogue.
